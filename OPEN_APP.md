### 🖥️ Option 1: Launch the Native Desktop App (Recommended)
You now have a dedicated **PersonalGPT** Desktop application installed on your PC:

1. Look on your **Windows Desktop** for the **PersonalGPT** icon shortcut:
   - Double-click **`PersonalGPT`** directly on your Desktop!
2. Or run **`PersonalGPT.vbs`** in `C:\Users\^_^\Music\ANTIGRAVITY\personal gpt`:
   - It silently boots up the unified AI backend in the background (no console windows).
   - Launches a dedicated, high-performance standalone app window (no address bars, no browser tabs, looking exactly like an installed native software).

---

### 🌐 Option 2: Open in Any Browser
If you prefer running in your standard web browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox):
